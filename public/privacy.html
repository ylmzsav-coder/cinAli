Word Okey Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use Word Okey (web version, Android and iOS apps), why it is used, who it is shared with and what your rights are. It also serves as the privacy notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Data controller

  • The data controller is Yılmaz Savaş (owner of the Word Okey app). Address: Güneştepe Mah. Şehit Özcan Canik Sk. Fazilet Apt. No: 8 İç Kapı No: 14, Güngören / İstanbul.
  • For any privacy question or request, write to [to be added] or use the "Feedback" section in the app.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your player name; your e-mail address if you sign in with e-mail (your password is kept encrypted by the sign-in service and we cannot see it); the name and account ID shared by Facebook if you sign in with Facebook; your optional gender preference; messages you send through the Feedback section.
  • Information created while playing: your account ID, game statistics (wins, losses, best words), chip balance and chip transactions, gifts sent and received, friends list, friend requests and table invitations, tables you sat at.
  • VIP Room chat: the messages you write, together with the sending account, player name, table and time. Messages caught by the filter are also kept as violation records.
  • Reports: when you report a player or are reported, the report and the latest messages at that table.
  • Automatically collected information: your IP address, connection and last-seen times (for online status).
  • Purchases: VIP membership and chips are bought through Google Play or the App Store. Your card and payment details never reach us; we only keep the purchase record (product, date, order number).
  • What we do not collect: your contacts, location, photo gallery, microphone or camera. Profile photos cannot be uploaded in the app. If you sign in with Facebook, your Facebook profile picture is shown as your profile picture; if you sign in with email or play as a guest, the first letter of your name is shown instead.

3. How we use it

  • Providing the game: creating your account, matching you with tables and opponents, managing your chips, gifts and VIP membership.
  • Social features: friends list, online status, table invitations and VIP Room chat.
  • Safety and fair play: preventing cheating, fraud and abuse; filtering profanity and illegal content; reviewing reports; restricting accounts that break the rules.
  • Legal obligations: keeping the records required by law (including Turkish Law No. 5651) and responding to requests from competent authorities.
  • Support and improvement: answering feedback, fixing bugs and improving the game.
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles and we never sell it.

4. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the game and the services you buy.
  • Legal obligation: retention of chat and connection records, requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interest: security, preventing cheating and abuse, improving the game.
  • Consent: information you choose to share (for example gender preference). You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other players: your player name, profile letter, VIP badge, scores and statistics are visible to players at your table; your online and at-table status is visible to your friends. VIP Room messages are seen only by the players at that table.
  • Service providers: the game runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database and server functions); purchases are processed by Google Play / the App Store; if you sign in with Facebook, sign-in happens through Facebook. These companies process data only to provide their services and under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities: if a court, prosecutor or competent authority makes a lawful request, the relevant records are shared with them.
  • Apart from this we do not share, sell or rent your data to anyone.

6. International transfers

  • Data is hosted on Google Firebase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Google and other providers may process data in other countries.
  • Such transfers rely on the safeguards required by law and the providers' standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

  • Account data, statistics, chip and gift records: until your account is deleted.
  • VIP Room chat logs: 2 years, then deleted automatically.
  • Violation and report records: during the review and for the limitation period of any legal proceedings.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by tax and commercial law.
  • Expired data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

8. Stored on your device

  • Your settings (language, sound, music), the link to your Facebook profile picture, on-device statistics and unfinished game are stored only on your device (browser/app storage).
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can remove this local data by clearing the app's data.

9. Security

  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Chips, gifts and game moves are verified on the server; other players' racks, chat logs and reports are closed to players.
  • Only an authorised administrator can access chat and violation logs, and administrator actions are logged.
  • No system is completely secure; if a breach occurs we will inform you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Children

  • Word Okey is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that we have, we delete it.
  • VIP Room chat is for people aged 18 and over. Under-18s should use the app with the permission of a parent or guardian.

11. Your rights

  • You have the right to know whether your data is processed, to request information, to learn the purpose of processing and the recipients, to have incomplete or wrong data corrected, deleted or destroyed, to object to results of automated processing and to claim compensation.
  • If you live in the European Union you also have the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection.
  • Send all requests, including a request to delete your account and data, to [to be added] or through the Feedback section of the app. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days. Records we are legally required to keep (such as chat logs) are kept until that period ends.
  • If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to your data protection authority.

12. Changes

  • We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published in the app and on this page; we announce important changes in the app.
