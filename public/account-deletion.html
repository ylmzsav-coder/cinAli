Word Okey Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: 23 November 2026

You can delete your Word Okey account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, even if the app is not installed.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  • Open Word Okey (Android, iOS or the web version: ylmzsav-coder.github.io/cinAli).
  • Tap "My profile" in the main menu, then choose "⚙️ Settings".
  • Tap "Delete Word Okey account" at the top and confirm with "Yes, delete my account".
  • If you are sitting at a table, leave it first. Deletion happens immediately.

2. If the app is not installed

  • Open the web version in a browser and sign in the same way you created your account (e-mail or Facebook), then follow the steps in section 1.
  • If you cannot sign in, send an e-mail with the subject "Account deletion request" to [to be added], stating your player name and the e-mail address or sign-in method you used.
  • We may ask for additional information to confirm the account is yours. Requests are completed free of charge within 30 days.

3. Data that is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in data (player name, e-mail, sign-in ID).
  • Your chip balance and chip transactions, gifts, VIP membership and statistics.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, table invitations, blocked players and settings.
  • A deleted account cannot be restored; chips, gifts and remaining VIP time are not refunded.

4. Data kept for a limited time

  • VIP Room chat logs: 2 years because of the retention obligation under Turkish Law No. 5651, then deleted automatically.
  • Rule violation and report records: for the review and the limitation period of any legal proceedings.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by tax and commercial law.
  • Local data on your device is cleared when you delete in the app; uninstalling the app also removes it.
