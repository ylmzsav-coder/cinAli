Word Okey Community Guidelines

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Welcome to Word Okey! This is the table for everyone who plays okey with letters, builds words, chats and makes new friends. We have a few rules so that everyone can play happily and safely. They apply in games, in VIP Room chat, in player names and in profiles.

1. Be a good sport

  • Winning and losing are both part of the game. Congratulate your opponent and do not ruin the table when you lose.
  • Do not stall the game on purpose, pressure players outside the game or hold a table hostage.
  • Keep chat friendly: do not force anyone into a conversation they do not want, and do not insist.

2. No cheating, play fair

  • Modified clients, bots, exploit tools or sitting at the same table with several accounts are forbidden.
  • Fixing games with another player or losing on purpose to pass chips or gifts is cheating.
  • If you find a bug or exploit, report it through the Feedback section instead of using it.

3. No swearing or obscenity

  • Profane messages are blocked automatically; after three attempts your chat is disabled for 10 minutes.
  • Sexual or obscene messages and names are forbidden.
  • Your player name may not contain profanity or links; if it does, a "Guest" name is given automatically.

4. Zero tolerance for illegal content

  • Talking about drugs, weapons, illegal betting/gambling or any other crime, or offering to buy or sell them, is strictly forbidden.
  • Such messages are not delivered and are recorded as violations. On the second violation chat is disabled for 24 hours, on the fourth for 30 days; the account may be banned permanently.
  • Where necessary, records are shared with the authorities on lawful request.

5. Protect yourself and others

  • Do not share personal or contact details such as phone numbers, addresses, IBANs, passwords or social/messaging accounts, and do not ask others for them. Such details are blocked automatically.
  • Do not try to move players off the game, arrange meetings or persuade them to send money.
  • Nobody may ask you for real money, chips or gifts in exchange for anything. If someone does, report them.

6. No threats, harassment or hate speech

  • Threatening, persistently bothering or stalking a player is forbidden.
  • Content that demeans people or calls for violence because of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar traits is forbidden.
  • Any content exploiting children is strictly forbidden and is reported to the authorities immediately.

7. Be yourself, do not impersonate

  • Do not impersonate another player, a celebrity or the Word Okey team.
  • Do not share your account, or sell or buy accounts or chips. Chips are virtual game currency and cannot be exchanged for real money.
  • VIP Room chat is for people aged 18 and over; children under 13 should not use Word Okey.

8. Use gifts and chips properly

  • Gifts are for kindness, not for pressure. Do not ask anyone for anything in return for a gift.
  • You can send at most 3 gifts to the same player in 24 hours.
  • Obtaining chips or gifts by cheating or passing them to other accounts is forbidden; they are taken back if detected.

9. Report, block, tell us

  • Tap a player's profile to report them; reports and the latest messages at that table are reviewed by an administrator.
  • If you feel uncomfortable, turn off invitations and chat in your profile settings, or remove the player from your friends.
  • False reports also break the rules; use reporting honestly.

10. If you break the rules

  • Depending on the violation and how often it happens: the message is not sent, chat is disabled for a while (10 minutes, 24 hours, 30 days), the account is reviewed or banned permanently.
  • Serious violations may lead to closing the account without warning; chips, gifts and VIP membership are not refunded.
  • If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal through the Feedback section.

Remember

  • These guidelines are part of the Terms of Use. By using Word Okey you agree to follow them. We may update them from time to time; the current version is always published in the app.
  • Good games and lots of words! 🎲
